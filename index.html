02 / AI tools

Trading systems and agents we built.

Internal tools, built with the same discipline as the products. An edge has to be proven on data before money moves, and every system runs under loss caps and a kill switch that a human controls.

01

Kalshi weather trading bot

Trades daily high-temperature markets on Kalshi across the 16 settlement stations. A blended forecast engine pulls several weather sources, weights each by its measured error, calibrates uncertainty per station, and prices edge after fees against the live order book.

  • StatusRunning live
  • StackNode.js, Kalshi API, NWS and ASOS observations
  • GuardrailsLoss caps, instance lock, watchdog, Discord alerts
02

Stock and options trading system

A self-improving system on Interactive Brokers. A deterministic risk governor sits at the top: daily breakers, a portfolio heat cap, earnings blackouts, and a paper-to-live gate that needs both passing criteria and a human switch. A learning module runs in shadow mode until it earns promotion.

  • StatusBuilt through the live gate, trading paper
  • StackPython, IBKR API, SQLite, Telegram
  • GuardrailsGovernor outranks every model, no sizing to goals
03

Prediction-market behavior monitor

Watches the live Polymarket trade firehose and flags behavior worth attention: large bets from brand-new wallets, trades outsized against a market's liquidity, prices far from standing odds, and repeat whales. Every signal is logged and scored against what the market did next, so nothing is claimed that the data cannot back.

  • StatusMonitoring and alerts, web app in progress
  • StackPython, Polymarket public APIs, Next.js, Supabase
  • GuardrailsAlerts only, never holds funds or keys
04

Kalshi sports betting bot

An NFL player-prop parlay bot for Kalshi, gated by phase. The edge on high-probability legs is being proven on settled markets with public data before any leg selection, paper trading, or live orders. Projections, injuries, Vegas lines, and usage data feed the later phases.

  • StatusResearch phase, proving the edge
  • StackPython, Kalshi public API, nflverse, Sleeper
  • GuardrailsPer-parlay and weekly loss caps, dry-run by default

05 / Questions

Common questions, direct answers.

Do you take on outside work, or only build your own?
Both. Most of our time goes into our own products. We also take on a small number of build-and-operate engagements each year, plus advisory for teams that want guidance without a build.
What does "answer engine optimization" mean?
Getting your business cited in answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity. More people now ask an AI instead of scrolling results. We structure content, data, and schema so those engines pull from you.
How do you improve return on ad spend?
The whole funnel has to work, so we tighten targeting and creative, fix the landing pages and tracking in between, and send paid traffic only to pages built to convert.
Do you operate things ongoing, or hand off after launch?
Operating is the default. We run our own products daily, and we offer the same managed operations for anything we build: monitoring, content, search upkeep, performance tuning, and steady iteration.
Do you push specific vendors or platforms?
No. We build on whatever fits your stack, budget, and goals, and we take no referral fees. Our incentive is your results.
How is data handled?
Every engagement starts with a data-handling review. We use reputable platforms with proper data-processing terms, apply access controls and PII handling, and write a plain-English policy your team can follow.
