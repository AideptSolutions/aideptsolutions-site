02 / AI tools

Trading systems and agents we built.

Our own systems, run on our own capital, built with the same discipline as the products. An edge has to be proven on data before money moves, and every system runs under loss caps and a kill switch that a human controls.

Kalshi weather trading bot

Kalshi runs daily markets on the high temperature in 16 US cities. This bot forecasts each one by blending several weather models, weighting each by how wrong it has been lately, and bets only when its forecast disagrees with the market by more than the fees. It trades our own account, and the profit is the product.

  • StatusRunning live
  • StackNode.js, Kalshi API, NWS and ASOS observations
  • GuardrailsLoss caps, instance lock, watchdog, Discord alerts

Stock and options trading system

An automated trader on Interactive Brokers that we run on our own account. A risk governor sits above every strategy: daily loss breakers, a cap on total open risk, no trading into earnings, and a gate that keeps it in paper mode until both the numbers and a human say go. A learning module studies our own trade history and flags the habits that cost money. Revenue is simply what it earns in the account.

  • StatusBuilt through the live gate, trading paper
  • StackPython, IBKR API, SQLite, Telegram
  • GuardrailsGovernor outranks every model, no sizing to goals

Prediction-market behavior monitor

Watches every trade on Polymarket as it happens and flags the ones worth a second look: a brand-new wallet placing a large bet, a trade too big for the market's liquidity, a repeat whale showing up again. Each flag is scored against what the market did next, so the record is honest. We use it ourselves, and a web app with free and paid tiers is being built for other Polymarket traders who want the same alerts.

  • StatusMonitoring and alerts, web app in progress
  • StackPython, Polymarket public APIs, Next.js, Supabase
  • GuardrailsAlerts only, never holds funds or keys

Kalshi sports betting bot

Kalshi also lists NFL player props. This bot is looking for a durable edge in parlays built from high-probability legs. It is still in research: before it picks a single leg or places a paper trade, the edge has to show up in settled markets using public data. If it does, it trades our own account under per-parlay and weekly loss caps.

  • StatusResearch phase, proving the edge
  • StackPython, Kalshi public API, nflverse, Sleeper
  • GuardrailsPer-parlay and weekly loss caps
03 / In development

Built, or being built, and not yet launched.

Works in progress at various stages of development, each with real code behind it. Some are waiting on a launch gate, some are still being built. Nothing here is live for the public yet.

Game

CRE Tycoon

A multiplayer game where you play a commercial real estate investor: walk the map, haggle with vendors, buy and improve buildings, and climb a live leaderboard ranked by portfolio value. For people who like business and tycoon games and want one built on real property mechanics. The server does all the money math and dice rolls, so nobody can cheat from their browser.

The longer play is behavior and decision mapping. Every bid, haggle, hold, and walk-away is a decision under uncertainty, and the game records them all. Over many sessions that becomes a profile of how a player actually decides, across risk, loss aversion, patience, and negotiation style, which is rarely how they say they decide. The game is the front door; that decision data is the long-term asset.

OvationChain

Group celebration boards for birthdays, work anniversaries, and farewells: the online version of the card passed around the office. One person starts a board and shares a link, everyone adds a message, and the finished board lands in the guest of honor's inbox on the day. The twist is the AI layer. The guest of honor can be placed into face-swap scenes, and contributors can generate a custom image for their message instead of hunting for a GIF. Paid boards, with a free watermarked tier.

Monitor

Taiwan early-warning indicator

A monitor that watches for signs of military escalation between China and Taiwan before it reaches the headlines. It tracks public market data, physical-world signals like flight restrictions, and news flow, compares each to its own normal range, and raises a graded alert only when several unrelated signals move together. Built to position our own portfolio ahead of an event, not for sale. It runs on public data only and never places an order without a human approving it.

  • StageSignal engine and alert ladder built, backtest pending
  • StackPython, SQLite, public OSINT and market feeds
  • GuardrailsRecommend-only by default, no order sent without human approval
Trading tool

ES orderflow bot

A desk tool for trading S&P 500 futures. It reads the live tape and order book from CME, works out where large buyers and sellers are actually defending price, and puts those levels on a chart with alerts when price reaches them. It is for our own intraday trading and never sends an order; a human does that. Whether a level holds is measured by replaying it against history, never assumed.

  • StageEngine, dashboard, and replay harness built; live feed needs a data key
  • StackPython, FastAPI, Databento, lightweight-charts
  • GuardrailsAnalysis only, no order execution

05 / Process

How a product gets made here.

Four phases, from a question to a product that runs on its own. Each has a gate, so nothing ships before it has earned it.

1

Idea

Start with a real question: who needs this, what would they search for, and how does it pay for itself. If the answer is weak, the idea stops here.

2

Build

Design and ship on a modern stack, structured for search from day one, with clean tracking in place before anything goes live.

3

Launch

Deploy to production, connect analytics and channels, and confirm real people can find it and use it before calling it live.

4

Operate and grow

Monitoring, content, search upkeep, and tuning. The product keeps improving instead of going stale.

06 / Questions

Common questions, direct answers.

What is AiDept Solutions?
A product studio that designs, builds, and operates its own websites, apps, and AI-powered tools. Seven products are live today, plus the trading systems above. Everything on this page is ours.
Do you take on outside projects?
Occasionally, by referral, when a project fits what we already build. Most of our time goes into our own products.
Why does a product studio build trading systems?
They are the hardest software we make: live data, real money, and an unforgiving scoreboard. The discipline they demand, prove the edge first, cap the downside, log everything, carries into every product we ship.
What does answer engine optimization mean for your products?
People increasingly ask ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, or Perplexity instead of scrolling results. We structure each product's content, data, and schema so those engines can cite it, alongside conventional search.
How is user data handled across your products?
Each product collects the minimum it needs. Calculators like DollarShuffle store nothing a visitor enters. Where accounts exist, data sits with reputable providers under proper data-processing terms, with access controls and a plain-English privacy policy.
