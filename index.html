Frequently Asked

Common questions, direct answers.

If something isn't covered here, email AJ@aideptsolutions.com and we'll get back to you within one business day.

What does AiDept Solutions do?
We build and operate web-based tools, products, and services: both our own portfolio and done-for-you work for clients. We design, build, and launch them, then run and grow them, with a focus on winning organic search (SEO), getting cited by AI answer engines (AEO), and improving the return on your marketing spend (ROAS).
Do you build for clients, or just advise?
We build. We're operators first: we design and ship web tools, products, and sites, then run them. Advisory is available as a separate track if you want strategy and guidance without a build, but most engagements are hands-on build-and-operate work.
What is AEO, and why does it matter?
AEO is Answer Engine Optimization: getting your business cited in the answers from ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and similar tools. More people now ask an AI instead of scrolling search results, so we structure your content, data, and schema markup so the answer engines pull from you, not just your competitors.
How do you improve ROAS?
Return on ad spend improves when the whole funnel works, not just the ad. We tighten targeting and creative, fix the landing pages and conversion tracking, and connect paid traffic to pages built to convert, so each dollar of spend returns more.
Do you operate things on an ongoing basis, or just build and leave?
Operating is our default. We run our own products day to day, and for clients we offer managed operations: monitoring, content and SEO/AEO upkeep, performance tuning, and ongoing iteration so the product keeps improving after launch.
Do you push specific vendors or platforms?
No. We're vendor-neutral and build on whatever fits your stack, budget, and goals: Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, Claude, GPT, n8n, custom code, or off-the-shelf tools. Our incentive is your results, not a referral fee.
How is data security handled?
Every engagement starts with a data-handling review. We default to reputable platforms with appropriate data-processing terms, apply access controls and PII handling, and document a plain-English policy so your team knows what's allowed and what isn't.
