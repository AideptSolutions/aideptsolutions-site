Scope

This policy covers www.aideptsolutions.com, operated by AiDept Solutions, LLC. It does not cover the products we build and operate. Each of those has its own privacy policy on its own site, written for what that product actually does.

What we collect

There are only three ways this site learns anything about you.

  • The contact form. If you use it, we receive the name, email address, and message you type. Nothing is collected from the form until you press send.
  • Email. If you write to us directly, we receive whatever you choose to include.
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location derived from your IP address, and the type of device and browser you use. We do not see your name or email address through analytics, and we do not try to work out who you are from it.

The site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no purchases, and no comments, so there is nothing else to collect.

How we use it

  • To reply to you when you write to us.
  • To see which parts of the site people find useful, so we can improve it.

We do not sell your information, share it with advertisers, add you to a mailing list, or use it for anything other than the two things above.

Who handles it

A few service providers process data on our behalf. Each one sees only what its job requires.

  • Formspree receives contact-form submissions and forwards them to our inbox.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and serves the fonts the site uses. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security purposes.

We would also disclose information if the law required it, or to protect the site from abuse. That has not happened.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics sets a small number of cookies, beginning with _ga, that tell it whether a visit comes from a returning browser. The site sets no other cookies of its own.

You can block or clear these in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works exactly the same with analytics blocked.

This site does not respond to Do Not Track signals, because there is no agreed standard for what they should mean. The opt-out methods above work regardless.

How long we keep it

  • Contact messages stay in our inbox for as long as the conversation is useful, then get deleted. You can ask us to delete one sooner.
  • Analytics data is kept for the limited period set in Google Analytics, then discarded automatically.

Your choices

Email us and we will tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. There is no form to fill in and no fee. We will answer within a reasonable time, usually a few days.

If you live somewhere that gives you additional rights over your data, such as California or the European Union, those rights apply and the same email address is the way to use them.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us something, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. We will not quietly broaden what we collect.
